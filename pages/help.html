How to use DairyERP demo.

Quick start

  1. Create account from the Register page.
  2. Add farmers & customers in their respective master screens.
  3. Start entering daily milk in Milk collection.
  4. Record feed issues, sales and payments.
  5. View totals and export from Dashboard & Reports.

This demo uses only your browser’s storage, so try it freely. For real deployment, plug into your Firebase or backend.
