Privacy Policy

How DairyERP handles your data.

DairyERP is a demo dairy management web app created for managing milk, feed and payments. All data you enter is stored in your browser’s local storage (and later optionally in your own Firebase project).

We do not collect or store any personal data on external servers by default.

Data stored in browser

  • Farmer and customer master data
  • Milk collection entries
  • Feed stock and issues
  • Sales and payments

You can clear all this data anytime by clearing browser data or using reset options (once implemented).

For a real production deployment, connect this front-end to your own secure backend / Firebase project and configure proper authentication and database rules.
